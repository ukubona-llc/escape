Five stages.
One geodesic.

Language → Science → Art → Life → Meaning. Each stage a local minimum. Each title of escape a preposition in the grammar of overcoming. Meaning is not arrival — it is the metric tensor that makes the others measurable. What others fail to say in a book, said here in one page.

The five stages — local minima and the one escape

I Language
Nietzsche: Birth of Tragedy + Untimely Meditations

Nietzsche still inside philology, speaking the borrowed vocabulary of Schopenhauer and Wagner. The tools are linguistic, the problem is aesthetic, the instrument is classical scholarship. This is the trap he writes from, not yet through. Every system begins here — in naming, in taxonomy, in the syntax of what can be said.

II Science
Nietzsche: Human All Too Human + Dawn + Gaya Scienza

The free spirit period. He picks up the scalpel of Enlightenment critique, turns it on Wagner, on metaphysics, on morality. Science as the local minimum that feels like liberation until you notice it's still a counting operation — okubala masquerading as kulaba. The Socratic trap: mistaking measurement for sight.

local minimum
↓
III Art
Nietzsche: Thus Spoke Zarathustra

The most explicitly aesthetic work in the canon. Prophecy, poetry, song, parable. The eternal recurrence announced not as argument but as vision. Existence justified as aesthetic phenomenon. And also the most misunderstood — read as religion, consumed as narcotic. Art as local minimum: it can become another Romanticism, another consolation, another trap with beautiful walls.

local minimum
↓
IV Life
Nietzsche: Beyond Good & Evil

"Beyond" is the preposition of escape. But what's being escaped isn't a doctrine — it's the deepest attractor basin in Western civilization: the moral interpretation of existence. The basin that makes suffering wrong, makes life something to be justified rather than affirmed. Escaping good and evil isn't a logical refutation. It's a physiological event. You can only do it from the body, from health, from what he calls overflowing life. Stages I–III were all defending this minimum without knowing it.

escape of the meta-minimum
↓
V Meaning
Nietzsche: Genealogy + Twilight + Ecce Homo — Ukubona: witnessing

The retrospective gaze. Not discovery but witnessing. The Genealogy shows how the moral basin was constructed — not to destroy morality but to see it clearly, which is already beyond it. Twilight smashes idols. Ecce Homo: "I placed the crown on myself." Not arrived at meaning — became it. No distinction anymore between the work and the one who worked. Statet = Σ Eventi. The autobiography as proof. The life as argument. Not the bottom of the bowl — the metric tensor that makes the other four measurable.

meta-coordinate · not terminal

The canon as trajectory

I. Birth of Tragedy, Untimely Meditations Language
II. Dawn, Human All Too Human, Gaya Scienza Science
III. Thus Spoke Zarathustra Art
IV. Beyond Good & Evil — the title is the escape Life
V. Genealogy, Twilight of the Idols, Ecce Homo Meaning
The structure beneath the structure
"Beyond Good & Evil doesn't escape a local minimum. It escapes the minimum that all the other local minima were defending."

Stages I–III (Language, Science, Art) were each, in their captured forms, downstream of moral interpretation — sneaking in the assumption that suffering requires justification, that existence needs to be earned. IV names the frame. V witnesses from outside it. The canon is not a bibliography. It is a lived trajectory.
